Pointer desktop preview uses Python, Pillow, PyInstaller, PySide6-Essentials,
Shiboken6 and Qt Core/Gui/Widgets. The corresponding notices accompany this file.

Qt and Qt for Python are used under LGPLv3. They are unmodified shared libraries.
The portable ZIP exposes the dynamically linked DLLs in _internal/PySide6.
Users may replace these libraries with compatible modified versions and rebuild
Pointer from its source for debugging those modifications. Pointer does not
prevent reverse engineering for debugging changes to the LGPL libraries.

Exact versions are pinned in requirements/runtime.txt and requirements/build.txt.
